Ezkiel Notch is on Windows. Ezkiel the lion now lives at the top of your PC's screen too: hover (or click, or press Ctrl+Alt+N) and the island opens with the same rooms as the Mac.

- **Den, Hunt, Scratch, Purr, Stash, Oasis, Roar and Settings**, in English or Bahasa Indonesia.
- **Notch or Island:** hang from the top edge, or float like the Dynamic Island (Small, Medium or Large, three gaps, a shadow). **Ezkiel's lion eyes** in the closed island blink, nap, squint and glow when an AI agent needs you.
- **Purr + Chorus:** anything that plays through Windows' media controls (Spotify, Media Player, browsers), with artwork, seeking and synced lyrics.
- **Roar:** unread counts from WhatsApp, Teams, Outlook and any app that notifies you. Only the numbers, no permission. **Scout:** Claude Code and Codex tell you when they finish or need you (connect them in Settings).
- **Stash + Pounce:** drop files on the island, drag them out anywhere, share them (Nearby sharing included). New screenshots and downloads peek with Copy · Stash · Show.
- **Rally:** your next meeting with Join, from your calendar's private iCal address (Google, Outlook, iCloud). **Weather** for your city, **Prowl** dots while a camera or microphone is on, **Mirror** to check yourself before a call.
- **Whiskers:** Caps Lock, keyboard language, Wi-Fi, headphones and battery. **The tray lion** opens the island and Settings.
- **Same data as the Mac:** `data.json` moves between Mac and Windows as it is (tasks, notes, water, focus history, settings).
- **Updates in one click:** Settings › About → Update now (checks the download's SHA-256 first).

Windows 10 (2004) or 11, x64 or ARM64. Download the zip for your PC, unzip it anywhere and run `EzkielNotch.exe`. This Preview isn't signed yet, so Windows asks the first time: **More info → Run anyway**. Turn on "Launch at login" in Settings to have the lion at every start.
